# Full Sweet  


Welcome to my baking journal! I make desserts with less sugar and just as much satisfaction. Please try it out if you would like a healthier version!


This is a personal website where I share the low-sugar bakes and recipes I test in my own kitchen. The idea is simple: cut the refined sugar without cutting the enjoyment. Every recipe is tested side by side against its full-sugar version, and only makes the cut if it still tastes like a real treat.


## What's on the site
- **Recipes** — interactive recipe cards you can tap open for the full method and an ingredient list that scales to whatever batch size you want. 
- **Why less sugar** — the approach behind the bakes: leaning on fruit, dates, browned butter, and a little salt so flavor does the work sugar used to. 
- **Journal** — notes, experiments, and lessons from the kitchen (including the failures).
