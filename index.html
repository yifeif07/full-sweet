Less sugar. Not less joy.

Most "healthy" desserts taste like an apology. Half Sweet is the opposite — I cut refined sugar by leaning on fruit, dates, browned butter, and a little salt, so flavor does the work sugar used to. Every recipe here is tested side by side against its full-sugar version, and only makes the cut if it tastes like dessert, not a compromise.
